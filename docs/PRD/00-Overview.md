# What is Wonderer?

Wonderer is a modern, student-first platform that helps university students discover, explore and participate in events, trips, workshops, competitions, clubs and communities happening around them.

Instead of following dozens of Instagram pages, WhatsApp groups and Telegram channels, students can discover everything from one trusted platform.

Wonderer aims to become the default discovery platform for campus life.

---

# Vision

Every student should always know:

- What is happening
- Where it is happening
- Who is organizing it
- Whether it is trustworthy
- How to participate

without relying on social media algorithms.

---

# Mission

Create India's largest student discovery platform that makes college life more engaging, social and memorable.

---

# Core Philosophy

Wonderer is not another event listing website.

Wonderer builds a trusted ecosystem where students can:

- Discover
- Connect
- Explore
- Build Communities
- Create Memories

Everything revolves around trust, simplicity and discovery.

---

# The Problem

Students currently discover opportunities through:

- Instagram
- WhatsApp Groups
- Telegram
- Friends
- Club announcements

This creates several problems:

- Students miss opportunities.
- Information is scattered.
- Fake organizers exist.
- No centralized platform.
- No personalization.
- Difficult to discover communities.

---

# The Solution

Wonderer centralizes student life into one platform.

Students can discover:

- Events
- Trips
- Workshops
- Competitions
- Clubs
- Communities
- Campus Activities

Everything is personalized based on interests.

---
