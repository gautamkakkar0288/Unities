# Apple (Reference)

## What to Reference
- Restraint — minimal color, maximum clarity
- Confident, bold typography for headings
- Generous whitespace as a design feature, not empty space
- Purposeful, subtle motion that clarifies rather than decorates
- High attention to detail in spacing and alignment
